How to order delivery

1. Open Uber Eats using the card above. 2. Enter your address on the platform. 3. Check available stores, total cost, delivery fees and estimated arrival time before confirming. Availability varies by area and merchant.

For Sirena and Jumbo, always check whether your branch offers online shopping, pickup or delivery. IslaViva RD does not accept payments or process orders.

How to compare deals fairly

Compare products of the same size and packaging, check unit prices and any delivery fees. Advertised discounts may not apply at every branch in Higüey, Bávaro or Punta Cana.
